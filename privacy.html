Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

This policy explains how HyperWare Solutions processes personal data when you visit skerjanc.org or contact us, in accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the Slovenian Personal Data Protection Act (ZVOP-2).

1. Data controller

HyperWare Solutions, Nejc Škerjanc, s.p.
Kranj, Slovenia
Email: info@skerjanc.org

2. Personal data we process

  • Contact form: full name, email address, company (optional), project type, project description, selected language and your consent.
  • Email correspondence: the data you provide when writing to us directly.
  • Technical data: when you visit the website, our hosting provider automatically processes your IP address, browser type and time of access in server logs, for security and operational purposes.
  • Language preference: your chosen language (EN/SL) is stored only in your own browser (local storage) and is never sent to us.

This website does not use cookies, analytics or any tracking or advertising technologies.

3. Purposes and legal basis

  • Responding to your enquiry and taking steps prior to entering into a contract — Article 6(1)(a) and (b) GDPR (consent and pre-contractual measures).
  • Operating and securing the website — Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest).

4. Recipients and processors

We share personal data only with service providers that are necessary to operate this website and respond to you:

  • GitHub, Inc. (USA) — website hosting (GitHub Pages).
  • Web3Forms — delivery of contact form submissions to our mailbox.
  • Our email service provider — receiving and storing correspondence.

Fonts are hosted on our own website; no data is transmitted to Google or other font providers. We do not sell personal data or share it for marketing purposes.

5. Transfers outside the EU

Some providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. In such cases, transfers are carried out on the basis of an adequacy decision of the European Commission (including the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or standard contractual clauses.

6. Retention period

Enquiry data is kept for as long as necessary to handle your enquiry, and no longer than 12 months after our last contact, unless a business relationship is established — in which case data is retained for the periods required by law.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection to processing. Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal. To exercise your rights, contact us at info@skerjanc.org.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the supervisory authority: Information Commissioner of the Republic of Slovenia (Informacijski pooblaščenec), Dunajska cesta 22, 1000 Ljubljana, www.ip-rs.si.

8. Security

The website is served exclusively over an encrypted HTTPS connection. We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal data against unauthorised access, loss or misuse.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page, together with the date of the last update.
