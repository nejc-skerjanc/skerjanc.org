Approach

A clear path from
idea to running system.

Every project follows the same disciplined rhythm. You always know what is being built, why, and what comes next.

  1. Phase 01

    Discover

    We map your goals, constraints and existing systems. You get a written scope and an honest estimate.

  2. Phase 02

    Design

    Architecture, data model and interfaces are defined up front — decisions documented, risks surfaced early.

  3. Phase 03

    Build

    Short iterations with working demos. Tested, reviewed code you can see progressing every week.

  4. Phase 04

    Run

    Deployment, documentation and handover — or ongoing support and development on a monthly retainer.

About us

People who take ownership.

Founded by Nejc Škerjanc in 2019, HyperWare Solutions offers companies something surprisingly hard to find: an experienced engineering team that takes full responsibility for a system — from the first conversation to years of reliable operation.

Our work sits where business processes meet technology. We build the software that runs operations, connect the systems that don't talk to each other, and bring order to codebases that have grown out of control.

We care about doing things properly: clear architecture, honest estimates, and code that the next developer will thank you for.
