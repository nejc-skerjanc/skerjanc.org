# Surface brief: homepage (index.html)

Scope: the single-page homepage of skerjanc.org (EN + SL). Mode: Persuade.
Audience: SME owners/managers, referred, non-technical, checking trust before enquiring.
Action: send an enquiry (contact form) or email Nejc. Proof: real facts only (2019, 5 services, 6 industries, 4 phases, portrait); no clients, testimonials or case studies exist.
Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, i18n via data-i18n keys, privacy.html and 404.html share styles.css.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Working with HyperWare is a marked mountain trail: you always see where you are, what comes next and who walks it with you. Refuses the cream-serif-italic agency brochure with eyebrow labels, metric strips and card grids.

OWN-WORLD: Slovenian trail signage. Cool limestone-grey ground, enamel signpost yellow owning large plates and the contact field, spruce-black ink, deep spruce green for the one quiet passage. Red-and-white Knafelc blaze reserved for "you are here": active nav item, the current phase, and the primary enquiry plate. Arrow-pointed yellow plates on a grey post; condensed heavy Archivo caps for plate lettering, normal-width Archivo for reading text.

STORY: The visitor reads the headline on the signpost, sees the destinations (services, industries, approach) with real counts, follows the trail down the page, meets Nejc, and arrives at the yellow contact board to send an enquiry.
