# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners and managers of small and mid-sized companies (mostly Slovenia / EU) who need software built,
connected or rescued, and who are usually not technical themselves. They arrive mainly through referrals
and LinkedIn, often having been told "talk to Nejc", and use the site to check that the business is real,
competent and trustworthy before writing an enquiry.

## Product Purpose

The website of HyperWare Solutions, Nejc Škerjanc, s.p., a software engineering business in Kranj,
Slovenia, founded 2019. It explains what the business does and converts a referred visitor into an
enquiry (contact form or email). Success = a qualified enquiry from a business owner.

## Positioning

A senior engineer, Nejc, who takes personal responsibility for a client's system end to end: design,
build, integration and years of maintenance. He brings in a small circle of trusted partners when a project
needs it, so "we" is accurate, but the client always deals directly with the people building the system.
No agency hand-offs, and the client owns 100% of the code, infrastructure and documentation.

## Operating Context

- Static website, hosted on GitHub Pages; English (default) and Slovenian.
- Enquiries go through a Web3Forms contact form to info@skerjanc.org, or by email or LinkedIn.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Services: bespoke software and web applications; system architecture and backend; system integration
  and automation (ERP, CRM, payments); cloud and DevOps; modernisation and technical consulting.
- Industries with experience: e-commerce, crypto and blockchain, fintech, healthcare, embedded systems,
  websites.
- Technologies: C#/.NET, TypeScript, Python, Node.js, React, SQL, Docker, Azure, AWS, REST/GraphQL,
  Solidity, Rust, Go, EVM/Web3.
- Engagement models: project delivery, long-term partnership, strategic advisory.
- All copy must exist in both English and Slovenian.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: HyperWare Solutions; legal name HyperWare Solutions, Nejc Škerjanc, s.p.
- Logo: "H" monogram in a rounded dark square with an orange dot (`assets/favicon.svg`).
- Voice: formal, precise and understated; no hype.
- A real portrait of Nejc (`assets/nejc.jpg`).

## Evidence on Hand

- Facts: founded 2019, 5 service areas, 6 industries, client ownership of the source code.
- Portrait photo of Nejc.
- **No publishable client names, logos, case studies or testimonials yet.** Do not invent any.

## Product Principles

1. Trust before persuasion: the visitor arrives already referred and needs reassurance, not hype.
2. The visitor should feel they are dealing with a real person, Nejc, rather than a faceless agency.
3. Plain language for non-technical owners; technical depth is available but not leading.
4. Make the enquiry easy and low-commitment.
